01

Why I made it

My wife and I wanted a shared grocery list that lived inside the conversation we were already having. ListWithMe began as a small solution to that ordinary problem.

When the original disappeared from the App Store, I eventually rebuilt it for the simplest possible reason: we missed using it.

02

What it does

The app keeps shared lists close at hand without asking anyone to install a second destination or move a conversation somewhere else.

Smart inputUnderstands quantities like “3 apples” while you type.
Shared activitySee what changed while you shop together but apart.
Useful groupingOrganize by aisle, category, priority, or due date.
Quietly presentLives inside iMessage instead of demanding attention.
What I learned

The software worth returning to is often the software that quietly became part of your life.

03

Where it is now

The current version is a complete SwiftUI rewrite with CloudKit sync. I am keeping the feature set focused and letting our own use shape whatever comes next.

Project notes

A personal app first. The technical decisions matter here when they explain why the experience became better.
